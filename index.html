    How to play

    Goal: capture the other player's Flag, or leave them with no piece that can move.

    1. Set up. Each player hides 40 pieces in their first four rows. You see your own ranks; the other player only sees the backs.
    2. Take turns. Red goes first. Move one piece one square forwards, backwards or sideways — never diagonally, and never into the two lakes.
    3. Attack by moving onto an enemy piece. Both ranks are shown and the higher rank wins. Equal ranks both leave the board. Afterwards the winner is turned face-down again, so try to remember what you saw! A small eye on one of your own pieces means your opponent has seen it.
    4. Bombs and the Flag never move. Anything that attacks a Bomb is destroyed — except a Miner (3), who defuses it.
    5. Scouts (2) can move any number of empty squares in a straight line, and attack at the end of it.
    6. The Spy (S) defeats the Marshal (10), but only when the Spy attacks first. Anything else beats the Spy.
    7. No piece may shuttle back and forth between the same two squares on more than three turns in a row.
